A policy is a promise with receipts.

RugShield is designed around the trust boundary: a live webpage is not automatically valid evidence.

01 / FUND

Reserve the pool

An owner funds the protection pool. Liability is reserved as users buy fixed-term offers.

02 / COMMIT

Lock the terms

Offers and evidence bundles are bound to canonical SHA-256 hashes before adjudication.

03 / ADJUDICATE

Let validators check

GenLayer validators retrieve sources and return COVERED, NOT_COVERED, or INCONCLUSIVE.

Consensus Claim Result

Live from the completed Full Consensus adjudication of rugshield-policy-002.

Verified flow / rugshield-policy-002

Evidence changed. Confidence did not fake itself.

The claimant submitted a SHA-256 committed evidence bundle. During Full Consensus, GenLayer validators independently retrieved the evidence pages. Both live pages produced hashes different from the claimant’s committed hashes, so RugShield marked both sources HASH_MISMATCH.

Because no VERIFIED evidence remained, the locked source policy could not be satisfied. Validators returned INCONCLUSIVE instead of incorrectly approving or denying the claim. The 1 GEN coverage liability remained reserved and no payout was made.

Auditable by design.

Every important decision has a place in the contract interface or its retrieval manifest.

Trust boundary

Only content whose live hash matches the claimant’s commitment can count toward the source policy or appear in accepted citations.

VERIFIED → hash matches
HASH_MISMATCH → content changed
UNAVAILABLE → source cannot be retrieved

Public methods that shape the protection lifecycle—funding, offers, claims, expiry, and surplus withdrawal.
